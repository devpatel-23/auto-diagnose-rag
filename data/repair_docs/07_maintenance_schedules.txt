VEHICLE MAINTENANCE SCHEDULES AND SERVICE INTERVALS
=====================================================

UNIVERSAL MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
--------------------------------

EVERY OIL CHANGE (3,000-10,000 miles depending on oil type):
- Engine oil and filter
- Check all fluid levels (coolant, brake, power steering, transmission)
- Inspect belts (look for cracking, fraying, glazing)
- Inspect hoses (squeeze — should be firm, not spongy or hard/cracking)
- Check air filter (replace if visibly dirty)
- Inspect tire pressure and condition
- Check lights (headlights, taillights, turn signals)

EVERY 15,000-30,000 MILES:
- Air filter replacement (sooner if in dusty environment)
- Cabin air filter replacement
- Tire rotation
- Brake inspection (measure pad thickness and rotor condition)
- Battery test (especially after 3 years)
- Inspect CV boots for tears
- Inspect brake lines for corrosion

EVERY 30,000 MILES:
- Fuel filter replacement (if separate unit — many modern vehicles have in-tank filters)
- Spark plugs (if copper type)
- Transmission fluid service (many manufacturers say "lifetime" — change it anyway)
- Coolant flush (test with coolant test strips)
- Power steering fluid flush
- Transfer case fluid (4WD/AWD)
- Differential fluid (front and rear)
- Inspect/replace PCV valve

EVERY 60,000 MILES:
- Spark plugs (platinum type)
- Timing belt replacement (interference engines — check model specific)
  Also replace: Water pump, tensioner, idler pulleys, thermostat
- Brake fluid flush
- Inspect motor mounts
- Inspect steering rack boots

EVERY 90,000-100,000 MILES:
- Spark plugs (iridium type)
- Coolant hoses replacement (preventive — hoses can look fine but degrade internally)
- Serpentine belt replacement (if not done sooner)
- Inspect wheel bearings
- Transmission fluid (again, regardless of previous service)
- Full brake system inspection (calipers, lines, hardware)

MANUFACTURER-SPECIFIC INTERVALS
---------------------------------

HONDA / ACURA:
- Honda Maintenance Minder system: Oil life algorithm triggers service.
  A = Oil change
  B = Oil + rotate + brakes + parking brake + steering
  1 = Tire rotation
  2 = Air filter, cabin filter, drive belt, pollen filter
- Timing belt (V6 models): 105,000 miles (CRITICAL — interference engine)
- Honda 4-cylinder (K-series): Timing chain — no replacement needed.
- Honda transmission fluid: Use ONLY Honda ATF-DW1. Other fluids cause shudder.
- Recommended oil: Honda 0W-20 (2010+ models)

TOYOTA / LEXUS:
- Very robust maintenance schedule.
- 2GR-FE V6 timing chain: No scheduled replacement.
- 2AZ-FE 4-cylinder: Known for oil consumption — check level frequently.
- Toyota ATF: Use Toyota WS fluid ONLY.
- Common issue: 3.5L 2GR-FE oil leak at VVTi actuator hose (oil pressure hose to cam gear).
- 4.7L 2UZ-FE V8: Timing belt — replace at 90,000 miles (with water pump).

FORD:
- EcoBoost engines: Carbon buildup on intake valves (direct injection) — walnut blast or solvent treatment at 60-80k miles.
- 6.7L Power Stroke Diesel: Fuel filter at every 15,000 miles (or 1 year). Very important.
- 5.0L Coyote: Excellent timing chain — monitor oil level, use full synthetic 5W-20.
- 6R80 transmission: Ford Mercon LV fluid ONLY.
- Common: Thermostat housing failures on V6 EcoBoost — coolant leak at front of engine.

CHEVROLET / GMC:
- LS/LT engines: Excellent — use 5W-30 Dexos certified oil.
- GM 6L80/8L90 automatic: Use Dexron VI ONLY.
- 6.6L Duramax Diesel: Fuel filter at 22,500 miles. Water separator drain every 15,000 miles.
- AFM/DOD system (active fuel management): Failures common — use oil catch can, consider AFM delete.
- Common: Oil consumption in AFM-equipped engines — monitor and use correct Dexos oil.

BMW:
- BMW Condition Based Service (CBS): Computer monitors actual wear.
- Oil service: Every 10,000-15,000 miles with BMW-approved oil (LL-01 spec).
  CRITICAL: BMW requires 5W-30 or 0W-30 LL-01 rated oil — generic oils may not qualify.
- N52/N54/N55 engine: Coolant flush every 4 years. Use BMW coolant.
- N54 engine: High pressure fuel pump (HPFP) failure common — has TSB/recall.
- Timing chain: N20 engine (2012-2016) known for timing chain stretch failure — preventive replacement recommended before 80,000 miles.
- Brake fluid: Every 2 years regardless of mileage (BMW requires this).

MERCEDES-BENZ:
- Service A: Every 10,000 miles — oil, filter, inspection.
- Service B: Every 20,000 miles — all of A plus air filter, cabin filter, brake fluid.
- Transmission fluid: 7G-Tronic uses 236.14 spec fluid — DO NOT use generic ATF.
- 3.0L BlueTEC diesel: DEF (AdBlue) refill needed — don't let run empty (engine deslates).
- Common: Air suspension failures (S-Class, E-Class, ML) — struts, compressor, air lines.

DIESEL MAINTENANCE (GENERAL)
-------------------------------
Diesel engines require:
- Fuel filters: More frequent than gasoline (every 10,000-15,000 miles typically).
- Water separator: Drain at every fuel filter change or as warning light indicates.
- Glow plugs: Replace as preventive maintenance at 100,000 miles (cold start issues if failed).
- EGR system: More prone to carbon buildup — cleaning or delete kits common.
- DPF (Diesel Particulate Filter): Modern diesels — needs highway driving for regeneration. City driving only clogs DPF. Forced regeneration with scan tool when restricted.
- DEF/AdBlue: SCR system — refill as needed (approx every 3,000-5,000 miles). Never let run empty.
- Injector cleaning: Every 30,000-50,000 miles recommended.

PRE-PURCHASE INSPECTION CHECKLIST
------------------------------------
Before buying a used vehicle:
Engine:
[ ] Check oil level and color (black/sludgy = neglect; milky = coolant)
[ ] Check coolant level and color (should be bright, not brown/rusty)
[ ] Start cold — note smoke color (blue=oil, white=coolant, black=rich)
[ ] Rev engine — listen for knocks, ticks, rattles
[ ] Check for oil leaks under vehicle
[ ] Compression test (for serious purchases)
[ ] Full OBD-II scan (check codes AND readiness monitors)

Transmission:
[ ] Test all gears including reverse
[ ] Note any hesitation, harsh shifts, slipping
[ ] Check fluid (color and smell)
[ ] 4WD/AWD: Test all modes

Body and Rust:
[ ] Inspect rockers, floor pans, wheel arches, frame
[ ] Check panel gaps and alignment (accident indicators)
[ ] Look for overspray (repainted panels after repair)
[ ] Magnet test for filler/fiberglass repairs

Electronics:
[ ] Verify all lights work
[ ] Test A/C and heat
[ ] Check all power accessories
[ ] Verify OBD-II readiness monitors are set (recently cleared codes = problem)
  Note: 0/8 monitors ready on scan tool = codes were just cleared — ask why

Inspection:
[ ] Professional pre-purchase inspection ($100-150 — always worth it)
[ ] Vehicle history report (Carfax or AutoCheck — check for accidents, flood, odometer)
[ ] Check service records if available
